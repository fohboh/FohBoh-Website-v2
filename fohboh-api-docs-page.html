Technical principles

Three commitments behind every endpoint.

01

Deterministic

No opaque or advisory logic. Results are computed by auditable governance rules. Same input, same output, every time.

02

Versioned

Stable, backward-compatible endpoints for production-grade reliability. Deprecations are announced with long lead times.

03

Auditable

Every certified record carries its evidence references, outcome, Trust Score and SHA-256 fingerprint, and anyone can verify a seal publicly.
