Metrics Governance Engine · The foundation

Every system of record assumes its data is true. MGE makes that assumption true.

MGE (Metrics Governance Engine) certifies data in an 8-stage Trust Loop, scores the evidence across 11 Trust Gates and seals every certified result in an append-only KPI Vault. No AI or machine learning touches certification: the same records always produce the same sealed result, byte for byte, with the same SHA-256 fingerprint.

The problem

Seven systems. Seven versions of the truth.

POS, payroll, inventory, delivery aggregators, payment processors, supplier systems, and accounting platforms all produce data independently — and nothing checks their work before it becomes the official record. Errors don't get caught downstream. They get amplified.

The Trust Loop: eight steps, no AI anywhere.

Every certification runs the same eight steps. Run the same records twice and you get the same sealed bytes and the same fingerprint; FohBoh's own quality checks certify every test twice and require the hashes to match.

1 · Resolve

Evidence is checked and mapped to canonical records.

2 · Govern

Every rule, formula and scoring version is pinned for the run.

3 · Calculate

Governed metrics are computed, money in whole cents.

4 · Reconcile

Sources are reconciled against each other: sales to deposits, statements to agreements, invoices to receiving.

5 · Test

The module's executable rules run; findings are proven or held.

6 · Score

The Trust Score is calculated across 11 Trust Gates.

7 · Determine

The outcome is set: CERTIFIED 85–100, VERIFIED 70–84, REVIEWED 60–69, FAILED 0–59. Missing essentials force REVIEWED; they never deduct.

8 · Seal

The result, narrative and evidence are hashed with SHA-256, signed and stored write-once.

MGE's governed corpus defines 198 platform rules. Module rule packs run on top of it, and each module release adds executable rules: today 46 execute across Sentry's three modules (10 for card fees, 15 for delivery fees and 21 for supplier invoices).
