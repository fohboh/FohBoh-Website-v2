The programs

One platform. Four essential financial control programs.

Sentry is the Certified Financial Controls Platform for restaurant enterprises. Powered by FohBoh's MGE™ infrastructure, it helps operators prevent financial losses, reconcile transactions, verify vendor compliance and recover money they're owed — with evidence behind every certified finding.

01

Loss Prevention

Find the leaks. Address the causes. Check for recurrence.

Identify unnecessary fees, billing errors, unexplained financial differences and repeated vendor discrepancies. Establish consistent financial controls across every restaurant.

02

AP Reconciliation

Check that you're paying what you agreed to.

Compare supplier invoices against purchase orders and agreed pricing, and against receiving records where they are available. Surface duplicate invoices, pricing differences, quantity and short-shipment differences, and unapplied or misapplied credits.

03

Vendor Compliance

Check whether vendor charges match agreed terms.

Verify merchant processing fees, delivery commissions and supplier charges against the applicable contractual terms.

04

Revenue Recovery

Find money you may be owed. Build the case to pursue it.

Document qualifying overcharges and discrepancies with supporting evidence. Give your finance team the information needed to pursue credits, refunds and corrections.

These are operating programs, not four new certification engines. All four run on the same MGE™ Trust Loop. When charges match the available evidence and the applicable terms, Sentry documents that outcome too — a clean cycle is a certified result, not a wasted one.

Trust Score · 11 Trust Gates

One score for the evidence. Four outcomes.

Every certification earns a Trust Score from 0 to 100 across 11 Trust Gates. A Trust Score measures the quality and consistency of the evidence evaluated — not the financial health of a restaurant or the amount of money recovered. Findings never lower it. A mandatory check that cannot run is never a pass: a hard Trust Gate can override the numeric score and return the run as REVIEWED. Every certification run produces a CAAR, but a CAAR alone does not establish a certified recovery entitlement.

CERTIFIED · 85–100 VERIFIED · 70–84 REVIEWED · 60–69 FAILED · 0–59

A CAAR is not an audit, review or other attest engagement, and expresses no opinion. Source data is provided and attested by the customer under electronic signature; FohBoh certifies it deterministically under the stated rules, and its conclusions are limited to the records and scope reviewed.
