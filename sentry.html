It isn't a dashboard. It's the evidence behind your claim.

Every certification ends in a CAAR (Certified Analysis and Audit Report), whatever the outcome. Not a dashboard you have to interpret: a sealed document that states the amount certified for recovery, the evidence behind it and the contract term it rests on, line by line, with the arithmetic shown.

Every dollar traced to evidence. Each finding names the contract term, the source records and the arithmetic. The rules are deterministic: same records, same result.
Sealed the moment it's generated. SHA-256 fingerprinted, digitally signed and written to an append-only vault. Anyone can check it through a public verify link; nobody can quietly revise it, including us.

What Sentry does not do

It certifies. You collect.

Sentry never contacts your vendors or collects money for you. It certifies what is owed and hands you the CAAR and its evidence pack. Its fee is 15% of the certified recoverable amount on a fee-bearing CAAR, earned when the CAAR is delivered. See pricing →

Trust Score · 11 Trust Gates

One score for the evidence. Four outcomes.

Every certification earns a Trust Score from 0 to 100 across 11 Trust Gates. It measures how complete, timely and consistent your evidence is, not how much money was found, and findings never lower it. Sentry issues a CAAR for every outcome; only a CERTIFIED CAAR that passes certification eligibility with certified dollars in it carries a fee.
