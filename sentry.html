The artifact · Powered by FohBoh | MGE™

It isn't a dashboard. It's the evidence behind your claim.

Every run produces a CAAR (Certified Analysis & Audit Report) documenting the outcome, the evidence and any limitations. When a discrepancy meets the applicable certification requirements, the report documents the finding and the basis for pursuing recovery — the evidence behind it and the contract term it rests on, line by line, with the arithmetic shown.

Every dollar traced to evidence. Each finding names the contract term, the source records and the arithmetic. Same evidence, same rules, same result.
Sealed the moment it's generated. SHA-256 fingerprinted, signed and written to an append-only vault. Anyone can check it through a public verify link; nobody can revise it, including us.
Built to survive the pushback. Evidence-grade, so the vendor argues about the money, not your spreadsheet. Sentry never contacts the vendor: your team sends the CAAR and collects.

Trust Score · 11 Trust Gates

One score for the evidence. Four outcomes.

Every certification earns a Trust Score from 0 to 100 across 11 Trust Gates. A Trust Score measures the quality and consistency of the evidence evaluated — not the financial health of a restaurant or the amount of money recovered. Findings never lower it. A mandatory check that cannot run is never a pass: a hard Trust Gate can override the numeric score and return the run as REVIEWED. Every certification run produces a CAAR, but a CAAR alone does not establish a certified recovery entitlement.

CERTIFIED · 85–100 VERIFIED · 70–84 REVIEWED · 60–69 FAILED · 0–59

A CAAR is not an audit, review or other attest engagement, and expresses no opinion. Source data is provided and attested by the customer under electronic signature; FohBoh certifies it deterministically under the stated rules, and its conclusions are limited to the records and scope reviewed.
