How we calculate your results

Potential findings = annual sales × loss estimate (× ongoing share after year one). Recouped dollars = findings × share recouped. CAAR fees = 15% of qualifying findings, earned when the CAAR is delivered, whether or not the vendor pays. Net benefit = recouped dollars − CAAR fees − $2,388. Return = net benefit ÷ (subscription + CAAR fees). Break-even findings = $2,388 ÷ (share recouped − 15%); break-even recovery = break-even findings × share recouped. At a recouped share of 15% or less, recovery alone cannot cover Sentry.
