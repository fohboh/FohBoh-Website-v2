Who can see what I open in the data room?

FohBoh.ai records each sign-in, PIN attempt and document opened, with the name and email address you entered, the time and your network address. Only Michael Atkinson can read that record.

How do I get access to a restricted folder?

Restricted folders (Financials & Cap Table, Intellectual Property, Legal & Material Contracts) open with a six-digit PIN that Michael Atkinson issues to you for each folder. Ask through the button above.

Can I download or share documents?

Documents open in your browser through a link that expires within a minute. Everything here stays subject to your NDA: please don't forward, copy or redistribute it.

How current are the materials?

Each document shows the date it was last updated. Michael Atkinson keeps the data room current through the process.

How long does my access last?

Sessions end after eight hours or when you sign out; PIN unlocks last for the session. Your access itself stays open until the date Michael Atkinson set, or until it is withdrawn.
