The Trust Score

Confidence in the process, not approval of the result.

The Trust Score doesn't grade the number — it certifies how reliably and completely that number was calculated. A good-looking figure on ungoverned data is the most expensive kind.

CERTIFIED 85–100 · VERIFIED 70–84 · REVIEWED 60–69 · FAILED 0–59

Restaurant example · bad outcome, high trust 95

Food cost at 45% — trust it.

Inventory complete, every invoice accounted for, formula verified across all 11 Trust Gates: CERTIFIED. This 45% is your real performance — fix the business problem, not data ghosts.

Restaurant example · good outcome, low trust 65

Food cost at 28% — don't.

Inventory five days stale, labor hours missing. The good-looking number rests on incomplete data, so it scores REVIEWED and MGE™ flags it before anyone acts on it.
