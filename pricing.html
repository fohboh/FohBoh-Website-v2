Before we talk.

Can Sentry and Cortex be scoped separately?

Yes. They are distinct applications with different purposes. A pricing conversation can cover either one, or both as a combined engagement.

Is the combined engagement available today?

Both products are in staging, with production release pending. This page invites the conversation; it does not indicate general availability or commit to contents, timing or terms.

What does a Sentry run deliver?

A Certified Analysis & Audit Report every time, including when no supported discrepancy is found. Financial assurance is the purpose; recovery is a possible use of supported findings, not a condition for receiving the report.

Does an engagement guarantee recovery?

No. Supported discrepancies in a CAAR can inform a claim, but recovery is never guaranteed. Your team decides what to pursue; FohBoh does not contact vendors or collect funds.
