Sentry · CAAR transaction fee

One fee, on certified dollars only.

When it appliesOnly on a fee-bearing CAAR: the outcome is CERTIFIED (Trust Score 85–100), the certification passes eligibility (Trust Gate 11), and the CAAR certifies a positive recoverable amount. VERIFIED, REVIEWED and FAILED CAARs carry no fee.
Rate15% of the certified recoverable amount.
MinimumNo fee when a CAAR certifies less than $500.
Billed onceEach certified dollar is billed once. A re-certification is billed only on new certified dollars, and a corrected CAAR that certifies less is credited back.
When earnedWhen the CAAR is delivered, not when money is collected. The fee is the price of the analysis and the CAAR.
No capThe fee is never capped: it stays proportional to what is certified.

Examples

Fee: $2,055.00 × 15% = $308.25

Below the $500 minimum: no fee

Not fee-bearing: no fee

Pricing FAQ

Is there a charge per certification?

Not for restaurant operators. Sentry and Cortex are flat per-location subscriptions, and test runs are never billed. Headless MGE partners pay a small toll per certification under their license.

Does FohBoh collect the money or contact my vendors?

No. Sentry certifies what you are owed and gives you the CAAR and its evidence pack. Your team sends the claim and collects. FohBoh never contacts your vendors.

Why is the CAAR fee earned on delivery, not on collection?

The fee is the price of the certified analysis, so FohBoh has no stake in whether a vendor pays. It is due when the CAAR is delivered, only on a fee-bearing CAAR, and never on a CAAR that certifies less than $500.

Do I get a CAAR when the result is not CERTIFIED?

Yes. Sentry issues a CAAR for every outcome. VERIFIED, REVIEWED and FAILED CAARs tell you what the evidence supports and what is missing, and they carry no fee.

How does the M04 sign-up offer work?

Take M04 Supplier Invoice certification when you sign up for Sentry, and M04's $199 monthly fee is free in months 11 and 12 for each location: twelve months of M04 for the price of ten. The offer never applies to the CAAR fee.

What happens when the Advisor allowance runs out?

The Advisor tells you, and your Owner can add more for the current month: in Sentry, 100 answers for $19 or 200 for $30. Allowances reset on the 1st. Certification never stops, and the Advisor never changes a certified result.

How is the subscription billed?

Per location, monthly in advance, at the start of each month. CAAR fees and top-ups are invoiced separately. Prices are in US dollars, before applicable taxes. Your contract is with FohBoh.ai, Inc.

What does the Sentry + Cortex bundle cost?

The bundle price is still to be set. Contact us and we will quote it for your locations.

Can I add products later?

Yes. Sentry is an upgrade for Cortex customers, and Sentry customers can add Cortex. Adding one product never changes the other's records.
