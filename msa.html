4. Intake and Chain of Custody

The intake channel is the chain of custody

The Console, and any data connection FohBoh has approved for Client (FohBoh Connect), are the only authorized intake channels; a data connection fingerprints each batch on receipt in the same way. Documents sent by email, messaging application, shared drive, or physical media are outside the chain of custody, cannot be sealed at receipt, and will not be admitted into an Intake Set. This is not an administrative preference. A record whose moment of receipt cannot be fixed by hash and timestamp cannot support the self-authentication design of the ExportPack.

4.1 Seal on receipt

Each file received through the Console or a FohBoh data connection is hashed using SHA-256 and timestamped in UTC at the moment of receipt, before any inspection, conversion, parsing, renaming, or movement. The hash, timestamp, authenticated uploader identity, assigned requirement, original filename, byte size, and seal sequence number are written to the Intake Ledger. The Intake Ledger is append-only.

4.2 Read-only treatment

FohBoh will not alter a Fingerprinted Source Record. Normalization, conversion, and enrichment operate on derived copies; the sealed original is retained unmodified and is the copy carried into the ExportPack.

4.3 Intake Closure

An Intake Set is closed when (a) every requirement in the Intake Manifest has at least one accepted file, (b) FohBoh’s completeness review has passed, and (c) Client’s Custodian has executed the attestation under Section 3.4. Intake Closure is itself hashed and recorded. Documents uploaded after Intake Closure are handled as a supplemental set and may require a superseding CAAR under Section 5.6.

4.4 Relationship to the ExportPack

The Fingerprinted Source Records in a closed Intake Set become the source-document components of the ExportPack issued with the resulting CAAR. The hash recorded at intake is the hash carried in the ExportPack Manifest, and the two must match. A mismatch is a certification failure and blocks issuance.

4.5 Records requests

Client may download its Fingerprinted Source Records, Intake Ledger, CAARs, and ExportPacks from the Console at any time during the Term and for ninety (90) days after expiration or termination, except that the ExportPack for a Qualifying CAAR is released as stated in Section 7.14.

5. Certification and Deliverables

5.1 Certification Pipeline

FohBoh will process each closed Intake Set through the Certification Pipeline. Rule execution, reconciliation, scoring, and narrative generation are deterministic and never use AI or any language model. Identical inputs and an identical rule registry version produce a byte-identical Sealed Record with the same SHA-256 fingerprint, and the rule registry version is recorded in the ExportPack audit trail.

5.2 Trust Score, and what it does and does not decide

MGE evaluates the Trust Gates and produces a Trust Score for each analysis and determines its outcome: CERTIFIED (85–100), VALIDATED (70–84), REVIEWED (60–69) or FAILED (0–59). FohBoh issues a CAAR for every completed analysis, together with its ExportPack and Custodian Declaration, whatever Trust Score and outcome that analysis achieves. The Trust Score is stated on the face of the CAAR.

The Trust Score and outcome never decide whether Client receives a CAAR; they decide whether a CAAR Transaction Fee is payable. A CAAR that meets the Fee Qualification Threshold and states a positive Certified Recoverable Amount is a Qualifying CAAR and is chargeable under Section 7.
